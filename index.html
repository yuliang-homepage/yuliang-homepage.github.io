I am currently a tenured Associate Professor in the College of Computer Science, Beijing University of Technology, China. My research interests lie in multimodal artificial intelligence, with a focus on intelligent perception, agent systems, and educational technologies .

Prior to this, I received my Ph.D. in 2022 from Beihang University, China, through the School of Computer Science and Engineering and the Shen Yuan Honors College (an elite program). I was a member of the Admire Group, supervised by Prof. Wenjun Wu. I also obtained my B.Sc. degree from Beihang University in 2017.
