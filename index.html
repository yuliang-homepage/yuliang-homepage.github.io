Yu Liang 梁堉

Email yuliang@bjut.edu.cn | yliang@buaa.edu.cn

I am an Associate Professor at the College of Computer Science, Beijing University of Technology, China. My research focuses on multimodal artificial intelligence, especially intelligent perception, cognitive and affective computing, LLM-based agents, and AI in education.

I received my Ph.D. in 2022 and my B.Sc. in 2017, both from Beihang University, China. During my Ph.D., I was affiliated with the School of Computer Science and Engineering (Admire Group) and the Shen Yuan Honors College (an elite program), under the supervision of Prof. Wenjun Wu.

I am always looking for motivated Master's students, undergraduates, and research interns at the College of Computer Science, Beijing University of Technology (Double First-Class and Project 211). If you are interested, please email me your CV and representative publications (if any).
