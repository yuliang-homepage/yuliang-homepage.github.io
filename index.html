Yu Liang 梁堉

Email yuliang@bjut.edu.cn | yliang@buaa.edu.cn

Faculty Page (CN) Google Scholar GitHub

I am an Associate Professor in the College of Computer Science at Beijing University of Technology, China. My research lies in multimodal artificial intelligence, with a particular focus on intelligent perception, agent systems, and educational technologies.

I received my Ph.D. in 2022 from Beihang University, China, through the School of Computer Science and Engineering (Admire Group) and the Shen Yuan Honors College (an elite program), under the supervision of Prof. Wenjun Wu. I earned my B.Sc. degree from Beihang University in 2017.
